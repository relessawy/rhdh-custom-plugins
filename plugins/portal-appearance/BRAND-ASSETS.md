# Brand assets

Argo, Jira, OpenShift and Splunk SVGs are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0). ServiceNow's symbol is from [VectorLogoZone](https://www.vectorlogo.zone/logos/servicenow/servicenow-icon.svg). Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; inclusion identifies an integration and implies no endorsement. Assets are bundled locally in `frontend/src/brands.tsx`; the browser makes no external image requests.

At tab-icon size, Splunk uses the greater-than mark from its wordmark to match
the visual footprint of the other symbols.

SonarQube uses the [Simple Icons 11.0.0 SonarQube mark](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.0.0/icons/sonarqube.svg) (CC0), in product blue with a transparent background. All tab marks share a 20px box and become white when selected.

For future tabs, use the product icon for product integrations. Generic capabilities must have distinct, meaningful icons rather than reusing another tab’s icon. Scorecard uses a checklist symbol.

- Dynatrace: Simple Icons `dynatrace.svg`, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/dynatrace.svg (CC0); product identification only.

PagerDuty: Simple Icons PagerDuty mark, green #06AC38, transparent SVG.

Kiali: official [Kiali UI logo](https://github.com/kiali/kiali-ui/blob/master/src/assets/img/logo-lightbkg.svg), Apache-2.0 repository. The SVG viewport shows the emblem; original paths and colors are retained.
