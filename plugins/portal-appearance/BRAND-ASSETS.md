# Brand assets

Argo, Jira, OpenShift and Splunk SVGs are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0). ServiceNow's symbol is from [VectorLogoZone](https://www.vectorlogo.zone/logos/servicenow/servicenow-icon.svg). Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; inclusion identifies an integration and implies no endorsement. Assets are bundled locally in `frontend/src/brands.tsx`; the browser makes no external image requests.

At tab-icon size, Splunk uses the greater-than mark from its wordmark to match
the visual footprint of the other symbols.
