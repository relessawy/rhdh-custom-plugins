WealthWise onboarding resources installed.

Export and append these settings to your existing RHDH values through its current Helm/GitOps process:
  oc -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} extract configmap/{{ .Release.Name }}-configuration --keys=helm-values.yaml --to=- > wealthwise-rhdh-values.yaml

In RHDH, open Self-service and choose WealthWise — Jenkins and GitOps.
The template creates {{ .Values.global.gitlab.owner }}/{{ .Values.global.application.name }} in GitLab. It must not already exist.
Jenkins will discover main through its two-minute poll. Argo CD needs that first build's images before the application can become healthy.
